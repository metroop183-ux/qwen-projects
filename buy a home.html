Questions Buyers Ask Most

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out to Ken directly — he responds within 24 hours.

Ask Ken a Question →
How much do I need for a down payment?
+
While 20% is traditional to avoid PMI, many loan programs allow as little as 3-5% down. FHA loans require 3.5%, and VA loans offer 0% down for eligible veterans. We'll help you find the best option for your situation.
How long does the home buying process take?
+
Typically 30-60 days from accepted offer to closing. The search phase varies — some buyers find their home in weeks, others take months. Being pre-approved and having clear criteria speeds things up significantly.
What are closing costs and how much should I expect?
+
Closing costs typically range from 2-5% of the purchase price and include lender fees, title insurance, appraisal, inspection, and escrow. On a $500K home, expect $10,000-$25,000 in closing costs.
Should I buy now or wait for rates to drop?
+
Timing the market is nearly impossible. If you find the right home and can comfortably afford it, buying now often makes sense — you can always refinance later if rates drop. We'll run the numbers with you either way.
Do I need a realtor if I'm buying new construction?
+
Absolutely. The builder's agent represents the builder, not you. Having your own agent ensures your interests are protected, helps negotiate upgrades, and typically costs you nothing — the builder pays the commission.
