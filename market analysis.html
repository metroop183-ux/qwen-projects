FAQ

Questions About Market Analysis

Everything you need to know before requesting your free analysis.

Request Analysis →
Is the market analysis really free?
+
Yes, 100% free with no obligation. We provide the analysis as a way to demonstrate our expertise and build trust. If you choose to work with us afterward, great. If not, you still have valuable data to make informed decisions.
How accurate is your pricing recommendation?
+
Our historical accuracy rate is 96% — meaning our recommended price is within 4% of the actual sale price. We use real MLS data, not automated estimates, and adjust for your property's unique features and current market conditions.
How long does it take to receive the analysis?
+
Standard delivery is within 48 hours of receiving your request. For urgent situations (like an offer deadline), we can often deliver within 24 hours. Rush requests are accommodated when possible.
Do I need to be planning to sell to get an analysis?
+
Not at all. Many clients request analyses out of curiosity, for refinance decisions, estate planning, or investment evaluation. The data is valuable regardless of your timeline or intentions.
What's the difference between this and Zillow's Zestimate?
+
Zestimates are automated algorithms that can be off by 10-20%. Our analysis is performed by a licensed realtor who physically reviews your property, uses actual sold comps (not listings), and provides nuanced recommendations based on current market dynamics.
Can I get an analysis for a property I don't own?
+
Yes! Buyers frequently request analyses for properties they're considering purchasing. This helps you make competitive offers and understand if the asking price is fair. Just provide the address and we'll analyze it.
