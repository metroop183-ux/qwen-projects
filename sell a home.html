Questions Sellers Ask Most

How much does it cost to sell my home?
+
Typical seller costs include 5-6% commission (split between listing and buyer's agent), closing costs (1-3%), and any agreed-upon repairs or concessions. We'll provide a detailed net sheet showing your exact proceeds before you list.
How long will it take to sell my home?
+
Our average days on market is 28 days, compared to the area average of 45-60 days. Proper pricing, staging, and marketing are key. Some homes sell in days; others take months. We'll give you a realistic timeline based on current market conditions.
Should I make repairs before selling?
+
Minor repairs and updates often provide excellent ROI. We'll do a pre-listing walkthrough and recommend cost-effective improvements that maximize your sale price. Sometimes it's better to price accordingly and let buyers handle major work themselves.
What if I need to sell and buy at the same time?
+
We specialize in coordinating simultaneous transactions. Options include sale contingencies, bridge loans, or renting back after closing. We'll create a strategy that minimizes risk and stress while protecting your financial interests.
How do you determine the right listing price?
+
We conduct a comprehensive CMA (Comparative Market Analysis) examining recent sales, active competition, market trends, and your home's unique features. We'll present data-driven pricing options with projected outcomes so you can make an informed decision.
