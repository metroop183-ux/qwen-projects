FAQ

Questions Owners Ask Most

Everything you need to know before handing over the keys.

Schedule a Consultation →
How much does property management cost?
+
Our fees range from 8-10% of monthly rent collected, depending on the service level. There's also a one-time lease-up fee (typically one month's rent) when we place a new tenant. No hidden fees — ever.
How do you screen tenants?
+
We run comprehensive background checks including credit scores, criminal history, eviction records, employment verification, and income confirmation (typically 3x rent requirement). We also contact previous landlords for references.
What happens if a tenant stops paying?
+
We enforce late fees per the lease, communicate immediately, and if necessary, begin the eviction process. Our legal partners handle court filings. We keep you informed at every step and work to minimize your financial exposure.
How are maintenance requests handled?
+
Tenants submit requests through our online portal. Non-emergencies are addressed within 24-48 hours. Emergencies (water leaks, no heat, etc.) are handled immediately. We use our vetted vendor network for competitive pricing and quality work.
Can I still access my property?
+
Absolutely. As the owner, you have full access to your property with proper notice to tenants (typically 24-48 hours per local law). We coordinate all access to ensure tenant rights are respected.
How do I get paid?
+
Rent is collected via our online portal. After deducting management fees and any approved expenses, the net amount is direct-deposited to your account by the 10th of each month. Detailed statements are available in your owner portal.
